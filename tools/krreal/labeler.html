    Labeling rules

    • The 4 corners go TL (top left), TR, BR, BL in the reading direction of the page. When the page is rotated in the photo, the top of the page still sets the order. The thick yellow line marks the page top.
    • Coordinates are pixels of the photo with EXIF orientation applied. A corner outside the photo goes in the empty area outside the photo.
    • For a curved edge, place 5 or more points between the corners and press Enter (7 or more points with the corners). Leave straight edges out.
    • A text region encloses all the text, running heads and page numbers included. Tables and formulas can be added as regions of their own kind.
    • Reference lines are 3–5 lines that are straight on the flat page: text baselines, table rules, vertical lines. Place points closely along a curved line.
    • For a two-page spread, place the corners of each page (+ New page). The left page is 1 in reading order.
    • Aim for 1.5–2 minutes per photo. The time spent is recorded automatically.
